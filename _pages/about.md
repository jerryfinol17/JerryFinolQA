Hi, I'm Jerry.

If you've made it this far, thank you. It means you've spent a few minutes exploring my work, and that alone means a lot to me.
This website isn't just a portfolio. It's my little corner of the internet—a place where I can share what I love, what I've learned, and hopefully help founders build products their users genuinely enjoy.

I've always loved taking things apart just to understand how they worked. Unfortunately... I wasn't always very good at putting them back together. More than once I ended up with a few mysterious "extra pieces" and a toy that never quite worked the same again.
Looking back, it's funny how those moments taught me something I still carry into my work today.
Every small piece matters.

A product isn't just a collection of features. It's hundreds of little decisions working together to create an experience.

That's one of the reasons I fell in love with Quality Assurance.

Not because I enjoy finding bugs.

Because I enjoy helping people build something better.

When I work with founders, I don't see "another startup."
I see someone who believed in an idea enough to spend months—or even years—bringing it to life.
That deserves care.
My goal isn't simply to point at what's broken.

It's to help founders deliver the best version of their vision, while helping users receive a product they can trust, understand, and genuinely enjoy.

Because, to me, Quality Assurance has always been about serving both sides.

**The people building software.**

**And the people using it.**

Outside of software, I'm a huge fan of books and video games.

Some of my favorite memories come from stories that felt so immersive they almost disappeared. Games where every interaction felt natural. Books that painted such vivid worlds I could almost smell the flowers or imagine conversations with the characters.
Those experiences stayed with me.
They taught me that people rarely remember software simply because it worked.
They remember how it made them feel.

That's the kind of experience I hope to help create.


> "Quality is never an accident; it is always the result of intelligent effort."
> <cite>John Ruskin</cite>

I love that quote because it reminds me that great products aren't built by chance. They're built by founders with a vision, designers who care, developers who solve difficult problems, and QA engineers who help bring all those pieces together.

My role is simply to contribute my small part to that journey.

That's why my philosophy has become incredibly simple.

> Understand first. Test second. Explain always.

Thank you for visiting my little corner of the internet.

I hope we get the chance to build something great together.
